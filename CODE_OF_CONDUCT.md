# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in pkgsentry to be a respectful, welcoming experience for
everyone, regardless of background or identity. We are committed to interacting in
ways that keep the community open, friendly, and healthy.

## Our standards

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. In short: be considerate and respectful, assume good faith,
give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the
community. Conduct that demeans, harasses, or excludes others is not welcome.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests, and any
other project channels — and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to
**security@cyfar.ca**. Reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's
privacy is respected. The maintainer is responsible for enforcement and may take any
action they consider appropriate, up to a permanent ban from the project.
